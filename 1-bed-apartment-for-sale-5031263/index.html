10 Block C Smithfield Market is an impressively spacious one-bedroom apartment, ideally positioned on the second floor of this ever-fashionable development in Smithfield. This stylishly decorated apartment offers excellent views across Smithfield towards the city centre, an idyllic retreat for those searching for a city centre home. The apartment also benefits from a designated underground parking space.

Apartment 10 is excellently proportioned and filled with natural light. The generous accommodation briefly comprises; a spacious, welcoming entrance hall with access to a large store room & hot press, an impressive open-plan living/dining room with a floor to ceiling windows overlooking the square below, with the modern kitchen positioned to the rear of the room. The bedroom is as equally generous with an abundance of space while also taking in views of the square below, while the main bathroom completes the accommodation.

The property boasts an exceptionally convenient location, within easy walking distance of TUD Grangegorman, NCAD, Blackhall Place, and the Four Courts. A variety of shopping options can be found nearby on Capel Street, Jervis Street, and Henry Street. Smithfield Village offers a great selection of cafés and restaurants, while the Luas line is right on the doorstep providing quick access to all areas of the city. Other nearby landmarks include Trinity College, Grafton Street, St. Stephen's Green, Stoneybatter, and the Phoenix Park all just a short stroll away.
